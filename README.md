# E3-GlucoCheck_G3

Project developed for the Digital Health and Biomedical Innovation BSc at the Faculty of Medicine, University of Porto (FMUP).

##  About the Project
GlucoCheck is an mHealth application concept designed for the longitudinal tracking of glycaemia, HbA1c, and nutritional habits. The project encompasses both the UI/UX product design and the rigorous clinical methodology required for its validation.

##  Key Components
* **UI/UX Prototyping:** Iterative, high-fidelity application design developed in Figma, focusing on patient accessibility and engagement.
* **Clinical Validation Protocol:** Structuring of a 6-month Randomized Controlled Trial (RCT) to validate the app's clinical efficacy and health outcomes.
* **Scientific Documentation:** Full methodological framework and research documentation deployed as a static website.
